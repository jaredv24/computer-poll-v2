          METHOD

          How It Works

          1RATING

          Team Rating

          Every team gets one rating, r, solved from wins and losses only. Opponent quality and win probabilities in later steps come from this number. It replaces v1.0.0's adjusted opponent win percentage (70% OWP + 30% OOWP).

          $$P(i \text{ beats } j) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-(r_i - r_j + h\,\ell_{ij})}}$$

          Here ℓ is +1 when i is home, −1 away and 0 at a neutral site. Ratings are fitted across every game, so a win over a team that beat good teams is worth more, however many steps removed. Each team also gets one ghost win and one ghost loss against a rating-0 team, the Colley Matrix correction, so unbeaten teams stay finite. All FCS teams are pooled into one rated team, fitted from three seasons of FBS-vs-FCS games.

          2ODDS

          Win Probabilities

          For a benchmark team B with the rating of the current #25 team, playing opponent j:

          $$p_j = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-a\,(r_B - r_j) - h\,\ell_j}}, \qquad 0.01 \le p_j \le 0.99$$

          The slope a = 0.81 and home edge h = 0.34 were fitted on 5,501 games from 2015–2025, each predicted from ratings through the previous week. The question is the committee's: how hard would it be for a good team to post this record on this schedule?

          3RÉSUMÉ

          Components

          Below, pj is the benchmark's chance of winning game j and dj = 1 − pj is that game's difficulty.

          Strength of Record 55%

          How unusual is this record for a Top-25 team playing this schedule?

          $$S = P(W_B > W) + \tfrac{1}{2}P(W_B = W), \qquad \text{SOR} = \Phi^{-1}(1 - S)$$

          WB is the benchmark's win total over this schedule, computed exactly. 0 is a typical Top-25 résumé; +2 means only about 2% of Top-25 teams would do as well.

          Quality Wins 20%

          Who did they beat?

          $$\text{QW} = \sum_{j \in \text{wins}} d_j^{\,2}$$

          Squaring makes elite wins count far more than routine ones. A win the benchmark gets 30% of the time is worth 0.49; a near-certain win is worth almost nothing.

          Loss Quality 15%

          Were the losses forgivable?

          $$\text{LQ} = -\frac{1}{L}\sum_{j \in \text{losses}} p_j^{\,2}\ \ (L \ge 1), \qquad \text{LQ} = 0\ \ (L = 0)$$

          The average badness of a team's losses. Strength of Record already counts how many.

          Efficiency 10%

          How well did they play per snap, adjusted for who they played?

          $$y_{g} = \mu + O_{\text{team}} + D_{\text{opp}} + h\,\ell_g, \qquad \text{EFF} = O - D$$

          Each game gives one row per offense: its EPA per play with garbage time removed. A ridge regression splits that into the offense's strength O, the opposing defense's allowance D, and home field. Shrinkage of 4 games pulls small samples toward average. Efficiency is net EPA per play, offense minus defense allowed. Success rate is adjusted the same way and shown for reference but not scored.

          This replaces the margin-based Game Control component. Per-play efficiency carries the same "how convincingly" signal with less noise from late scores and garbage time.

          4SCORE

          The Score

          $$\text{Score} = 0.55\,z_{\text{SOR}} + 0.20\,z_{\text{QW}} + 0.15\,z_{\text{LQ}} + 0.10\,z_{\text{EFF}}$$

          Each component is a z-score across FBS teams, displayed as 50 + 10 × Score. If two teams played and sit within half the median gap between adjacent top-40 teams, the head-to-head winner ranks ahead.

          Last-season carryover. Each team's final rating from last season, pulled a third of the way back to average, is blended in early: 50% of the score in week 1, then 40%, 30%, 20% and 10%, and nothing from week 6 on. Early opponent ratings also lean on last season, so an early win over a team that was good last year counts, and that pull fades on the same schedule. Teams new to FBS start from last season's average FCS team.

          The poll is rebuilt after every week of games from all results to date. The AP comparison for a week uses the AP poll released after that week's games.

          BACKTEST

          Does It Explain the Results?

          Retrodictive accuracy is the share of games already played that the higher-ranked team won, on final rankings for 2015–2025. The résumé core of v2.0 roughly matches the best simple methods and has the fewest severe misorderings.
